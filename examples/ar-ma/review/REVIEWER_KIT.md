# Reviewer kit — Arabic (Morocco) Starter

Every row in this pack is scaffold output. Review order:

1. `language/phonology.yaml` and `phonology/contrasts.jsonl` — segments and the contrast pair.
2. `lexicon/entries.jsonl` and `sentences/entries.jsonl` — forms, glosses, register.
3. `orthography/arabic.jsonl` — symbols, readings, mnemonics.
4. `curriculum/` — is the goal worth teaching, and do the activities measure it?

Approve by writing a `reviews.jsonl` record naming the reviewer and the date. The author model cannot write that record for its own content (REVIEW-001).
