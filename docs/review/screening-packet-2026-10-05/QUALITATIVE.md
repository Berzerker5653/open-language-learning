# Optional qualitative pass ("views on the whole thing")

Beyond flagging, we'd value the cloud model's open-ended assessment. Pick
any 5-10 batches and answer:

1. Do the glosses read like natural English definitions? Any systematic
   tone/precision problems?
2. For ru-RU: do the stressed readings look right (stress position)? Any
   sentences that read like machine-translation?
3. For ja-JP: any glosses that look like the AI confused similar-looking
   words (e.g. ほうけん feudalism vs 'health insurance')?
4. Are the assessment rubrics actually gradeable by a human observer?
5. What 3 content areas look weakest overall, and what 3 strongest?

Free-form prose is fine here; save as `results/QUALITATIVE-<model>.md`.
