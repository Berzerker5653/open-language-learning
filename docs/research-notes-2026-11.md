# Competitive research notes — 2026-11-17 (working file, cited)

## Duolingo (mainstream, gamified)
- **Max sunset Nov 2026**: Q3 shareholder letter (investors.duolingo.com, Nov 3 2026) — Max no longer sold; Video Call moving into Super (all Super subs by end 2026). Max was $29.99/mo / $168/yr; Super now $12.99/mo / ~$84-96/yr. Source: copycatcafe.com/blog/duolingo-max
- **Video Call with Lily**: real-time voice w/ AI character, topic tied to recent lesson, cross-session memory ("remembers what you discussed next time"), transcript + written feedback after call, replayable messages. Hidden "Closer" ends calls after N exchanges (calls are SHORT). Tap-to-speak button cuts awkward VAD waiting. XP counter during call. NO pronunciation score — feedback is on accuracy/complexity of responses. Sources: blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-max, practiceme.app/vs/duolingo-max
- **Roleplay**: scripted real-world scenarios w/ characters (ordering coffee etc.), type OR speak replies, see translations, feedback report after each session. Max only (Super availability unclear).
- **Explain My Answer**: AI grammar explanation after mistakes — made FREE for everyone Jan 2026 (commodity now).
- **Monetization/gamification**: free tier = ads + Energy system (replaced Hearts mid-2025), ~2-3 lessons/day before paywall. Streaks, XP, leagues, family plans.
- **Weaknesses per reviewers**: repetitive AI feels samey; not for advanced learners; same Lily persona across all languages ("spreads thin"); short capped calls; no pronunciation feedback; weak web/mobile parity (AI features mobile-only).

## Babbel (mainstream, structured course)
- $17.95/mo, 3mo $45.75, 6mo $80.70, 12mo $107.40 ($8.95/mo), lifetime $299.99; Group Plan ~$31/mo (Apr 2026). No free tier beyond first lesson per level. Sources: forbes.com, getslova.com/blog/is-babbel-worth-it, qlango.com/babbel-review
- **Babbel Speak** (open beta Sep 16 2025): AI conversation partner, scenario-based, hints + pronunciation feedback, can slow down/translate; feedback AFTER conversation, not mid-reply; mobile only; 4-5 languages.
- **Babbel Live (human classes) ENDED for individuals Jul 1 2025** — they replaced humans with AI. Market signal.
- Core: 10-15 min dialogue lessons, native-speaker audio (claim 100k hours), grammar explanations in L1 at moment of need, Review Manager (SRS), podcasts+transcripts, speech recognition in lessons, CEFR A1-B2, courses written per-native-language pair.
- **Weaknesses**: repetitive formula after months; conversation weak past A2; Russian/Polish/Turkish/Danish/Norwegian stop at A1/A2; billing/auto-renew complaints dominate Trustpilot; no spontaneous conversation.

## Busuu (mainstream, community + certificates)
- Premium $12.49/mo / $72.99 yr; Premium Plus $14.90-16.99/mo / ~$88-101 yr (heavy discounting). No family plan. Sources: qlango.com/busuu-review, busuu.com/en/premium-plans
- **AI Conversations** (Premium Plus, mobile, 5 langs): scenario role-play w/ CEFR-tagged levels, push-to-talk, analysis AFTER conversation (not distracted mid-speak), correct/improved message marks. Free users get limited tries.
- **Mistake Repair** (Premium+): turns your grammar errors into personalized exercises — notable feature.
- **Community corrections** (FREE, unique): natives correct your written/recorded exercises, 5 corrections per exercise; unverified natives → contradictory feedback. Closest to "teacher reading your work".
- **McGraw-Hill CEFR certificates** per level (own certs, not official exams; Russian only A1!).
- Speaking practice w/ video shadowing + pronunciation feedback (Premium+, 5 langs).
- **Weaknesses**: ads in free tier dominate complaints; tier confusion (AI behind Premium Plus, mobile-only, language-limited); level labels overstate ability; strict speech recognition rejects good pronunciation; Asian courses thin.

## Cross-cutting patterns so far
- Every major app bolted AI conversation onto a scripted core in 2025-2026; all give feedback AFTER the conversation (privacy/flow choice), not mid-reply (except Teacher AI clone-products).
- Pronunciation scoring (per-word/per-syllable) is the differentiator most reviewers say the giants LACK (Duolingo, Babbel Speak weak here; ELSA/Speak/Praktika strong).
- Explain-My-Answer-style instant grammar explanations became free/commodity (Duolingo Jan 2026).
- Human tutoring is being dropped by the majors (Babbel Live ended; Busuu live = business-only) — AI-only is now the industry default, vindicating LTS's fully-AI stance.
- Energy/hearts paywalls + streaks = the engagement engine; also the biggest source of user resentment (r/duolingo).
- Certificates/CEFR-level tracking = paid-tier retention hook.

## AI-native tutor apps
### Praktika (voice-first avatars)
- $10.99-19.99/mo monthly, $59.88-79.99/yr (48-67% discounts pushed hard); 17-31% of 1-star reviews = billing/trial. iOS rating 4.5-4.8, 100k+ users. 12 languages incl. Russian. Sources: practiceme.app/vs/praktika, lingopilot.com/blog/which-praktika-plan..., whichlanguageapp.com/apps/praktika
- 3D avatar personas with personalities (best-in-class first impressions in app-store pics); voice-first chat + structured lessons + goal tracking; 24/7.
- Complaints: conversation gets repetitive, limited deep feedback, weak for advanced.
### TalkPal (cheap, broad, shallow)
- $19.99/mo, $119.99/yr, 2yr $149.99 ($6.25/mo); free = 10 min/day chat only. 57+ languages. Sources: languatalk.com/blog/talkpal-review, saymei.app/guides/talkpal-review
- Modes: chat, calls, roleplays, debates, photo description, sentence drills — variety is its strength, depth its weakness.
- Robotic voices; inline corrections but NO post-lesson summary (only generic daily recap); no grammar lessons; no SRS; CEFR calibration off; content stays simple at C1; ASR mishears and marks wrong for things never said (Chinese tones not actually scored); trial→billing traps; 49% of Google Play 1-2 star reviews = free tier too limited.
- App-store 4.7 stars but ~half of 2026 written reviews are 1-2 star (star inflation ≠ satisfaction).
### Langua (most complete AI product, premium priced)
- Standard $12.50/mo annual ($19.99 mo, 30min calls/day cap), Unlimited $16.67/mo annual ($29.99 mo); 30-day refund ONLY on web purchases; free = 3 min calls/day. Sources: onlinecourseing.com/langua-review, lingtuitive.com/blog/langua-ai-tutor-review, whichlanguageapp.com/apps/langua
- Beyond conversation: podcasts, videos, flashcards w/ SRS, AI stories, imported transcripts, My Path curriculum, post-conversation FEEDBACK with categorized error lists, AI pushes beyond comfort zone; reading help (furigana/romaji) in some languages.
- Weaknesses: priciest; small user base (2.4k iOS ratings vs Praktika 161k); agrees when pushed back (sycophancy), apologizes for mistakes it didn't make; **no cross-session memory of repeated grammar errors — "every AI tutor shares this limit"** ← industry gap; feature overwhelm.
- Users at A2-B2 mainly; not a zero-to-hero course.
### Speak (curriculum-led speaking, OpenAI-backed)
- Premium $17.99/mo / $83.99 yr; Premium Plus $39.99/mo / $164.99 yr (Made-For-You lessons from your mistakes, custom review). 7 languages, English-focus market. Sources: speak.com, lingrow.io/blog/speak-app-review, speakshark.com
- Method: Learn phrase → drill speak → Apply in free conversation w/ tutor; real-time pronunciation + naturalness feedback. 4.8 from 63k ratings.
- Complaints: mic/ASR failures dominant (drops words, passes without hearing); free tier ≈ 1 lesson; lost progress reports; British English corrected toward American.
### ELSA (pronunciation-led)
- SKUs messy ($11.99-19.99/mo, $40/3mo, $99-159/yr varies by store/region). Sound-by-sound phoneme scoring, color-coded, articulatory guidance; strongest at A2-C1; drill library huge. Source: funfluen.com/learn/guides/elsa-speak-review
- Reviewer lesson: "red is a question before it is homework" — scores are practice signals, not truth; number-chasing trap.
### Loora
- English-only, interview/professional conversation focus; conversation-routine habit app. (pricing check pending)

## Input/reading lane
### LingQ
- Premium $14.99/mo, $119.99/yr (~$10/mo), 2yr $8.99/mo; Premium Plus ~$22.50-29.99/mo (AI voices, Lynx chat, AI-simplified lessons, Whisper transcription 6x); lifetime $229/language. Sources: qlango.com/lingq-review, storylearning.com/lingq-review, lingq.com/blog

## Cross-cutting AI-tutor weaknesses (= LTS opportunity map)
1. NO app remembers recurring grammar errors across sessions (Langua's own reviewer: "the correction has no stake in you being right... no memory of the mistake you have repeated for a month") — SRS-for-errors is an open gap.
2. ASR strictness/mishearing is the #1 complaint in every voice app (Busuu, Speak, TalkPal) — robust ASR + forgiving scoring = moat.
3. Level calibration is fake almost everywhere (TalkPal CEFR off; Busuu labels overstate; Duolingo plateaus B1) — real CEFR-anchored content = differentiator.
4. Content doesn't scale past intermediate (simple sentences at C1).
5. Trial/billing traps pollute reviews across ALL of them (Trustpilot dominated by billing) — clean pricing + web refund promise (Langua) wins goodwill.
6. AI sycophancy: agrees when challenged, apologizes for errors it didn't make — trust killer.
7. Star ratings inflated vs written-review sentiment; users increasingly distrust marketing screenshots (TalkPal tones case).

## Russian-specific lane (LTS's actual market)
### The money quote (verbamor.com, verified Sep-Oct 2026)
- "Every step up buys correction, not content. Content is cheap and abundant in Russian. Somebody noticing that you keep getting the genitive plural wrong is what costs money." ← THE positioning thesis for an AI app.
- Russian hard parts = cases, aspect, stress — NO app teaches case usage well; Duolingo Russian is NOT among Duolingo's 9 B2 courses (April 2026), plateaus low.
### Incumbents
- Rocket Russian: $149.95 L1 / $449.85 all levels, one-time lifetime, 60-day refund, 146-312 hrs, moderator-cut audio lessons + Google ASR 1-100 pronunciation score + grammar lessons + role-swap replay ("practice under pressure without embarrassment"). Best-reviewed Russian app 2026.
- Pimsleur Russian: $19.95/mo / $149.95/yr, 5 levels 30-min audio, graduated interval recall (=SRS ancestor), production-from-prompt with NO text; now has AI voice coach + driving mode; weak on reading/grammar explanation. Most-stickiest per r/russian.
- Anki: free; Lingo Llama Russian deck popular; per-INFLECTED-FORM cards are the pro move (genitive plural of человек = own card); FSRS not default; community decks lack stress/case context.
- Babbel Russian: stops at A1/A2 per own course list; $107.40/yr.
- RussianPod101: $4-23 list, heavy discounting, audio/video lesson library, no correction.
- NuanceDeck (new indie): 7500+ words A1-C1, one card per SENSE, stress on every word, case brackets under every sentence, aspect pairs linked, FSRS. No speaking, no course path. Closest philosophy to LTS on the market.
- Mishka (getmishka.com): built with a certified Russian tutor girlfriend; grammar+fluency over random words; r/russian recommendation.
- PlusOneLanguage: i+1 approach, level-tuned texts/dialogues with vocab recycled into later content — r/russian praised.
- Verbamor ($19.99/mo): turns YOUR lessons into flashcards, no course.
### r/russian sentiment
- Consensus: no app alone teaches Russian; apps = one tool. Pimsleur sticky for audio learners; "lack reading" is its complaint; Duolingo "fine but pointless alone"; heavy skepticism of word-memorization apps; tutors/in-person = gold standard for cases.
- Users explicitly want: stress marks, case explanations at point of need, production practice, reading support — and resent apps that teach "random words".
