# ADR 0002 — No Streak-Based Engagement

Status: Accepted

## Context

Streak mechanics can incentivize the learner to perform the easiest possible activity to preserve a number, decoupling product success from learning.

## Decision

Do not implement streak counters, freezes, daily-loss penalties, or equivalent loss-aversion mechanics. Support short meaningful sessions selected by learning value.

## Consequences

Engagement design must rely on capability, world/scenario progression, goals, and useful feedback rather than daily-presence pressure.

## Alternatives

Retrospective rationale added during handoff review; the accepted decision is unchanged. Daily streaks and trivial attendance rewards were rejected because they can be preserved without meaningful learning. An undifferentiated XP ladder would also obscure modality and capability evidence. Capability/scenario progression preserves the existing decision.
