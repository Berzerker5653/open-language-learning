# ja-JP reviewer worklist (2026-10-12) — A2 transaction batch (JA-A2-001)

Review-pending record for the A2 depth increment. Nothing in this batch is
verified: all 280 new items carry `status: draft`, and closing any of them is
qualified human linguistic work per `REVIEW_POLICY.yaml` — a machine agent that
generated the content cannot also clear its review.

- 136 new lexicon entries (`lexicon/{shopping2,city2,admin2,food2,health4,communication2,questions2}.jsonl`)
  and 144 new sentences (`sentences/a2-transactions.jsonl`, each with a cloze activity).
- 8 draft situations appended to `language/pragmatics.yaml` (post office,

## What a reviewer has to decide

**106 items carry an explicit `REVIEW_REQUIRED` question in `notes`** — verified
breakdown 12 "level check", 22 "duplicate/dedupe against earlier batches",
6 register/colloquial exposure, the rest item-specific wording questions. List
them with:

```sh
grep -h -o '"id": "[^"]*"\|REVIEW_REQUIRED: [^"]*' \
  languages/ja-JP/lexicon/{shopping2,city2,admin2,food2,health4,communication2,questions2}.jsonl \
  languages/ja-JP/sentences/a2-transactions.jsonl
```

1. **Level fit (12 level-check flags).** Whether the flagged words are really A2
   for our learners rather than B1. The flag names the concern on the item
   itself — e.g. 納付書 is the real convenience-store payment term but formal.
2. **Duplicates by form (22 flags).** The flagged `notes` name the older
   form/entry to compare against. If the older entry is good, delete the new row
   rather than keeping two near-identical records — duplicated depth is not
   depth.
3. **Irregular date readings.** 二十日→はつか, 三日→みっか, 十日→とおか appear in
   the new rows. Confirm the kana is what should be spoken: the bake input is
   the declared reading, guarded by `media_text_not_declared_reading`.
4. **Colloquial exposure policy (6 register flags).** The sentence-level flags
   name what they expose: the ちゃっ reduction, the casual contraction
   どこいった, and `attakai` (暖かい short form) in clerk speech. Decide whether
   A2 teaches casual register at all, and if so as recognition or production.
5. **Invented details.** Two items flag their own invention for a human call:
   "invented shop name as placeholder; acceptable?" and "naturalness of
   CDコーナー as a store-area name in current Japan".
6. **Source depth.** Every item declares only `jf-standard` +
   `ai-drafted-extension` — framework-level reference, no descriptor text copied.
   `verified` status additionally needs a dictionary/learner source per
   `REVIEW_POLICY.yaml`; `audit-licenses` passes at the current level and must be
   re-run after sources are added.
7. **One unit sits outside the contract system (pre-existing, not this batch).**
   `curriculum/units/000-onramp-greeting.yaml` is the only one of 786 units with
   no `generation_contract` block, so its allowed content is not scope-checked
   the way every other unit's is. Bring it under the contract or record why the
   first greeting unit is deliberately exempt.

## Audio

These 280 items are audio-addressable, so `validate` refuses the pack until they
are baked (`media_asset_missing`, ADR-0024). Baking runs on the pinned Qwen3-TTS
CustomVoice payload and every asset is recorded row-by-row in
`media/manifest.jsonl` (environment recipe and its reproducibility fix:
`docs/provenance/qwen3-tts-adoption.md`). Baked audio is regenerated media, not reviewed speech: the ASR round-trip gate
(`scripts/qa-asr.py`) runs per pack release, and a pronunciation an adult
reviewer disputes gets the item pulled, not re-synthesized around a reading the
reading itself has not confirmed.

### Bake outcome for this batch (measured 2026-10-12)

All 280 new items are baked. Two needed a second pass because the pinned LLM
voice ran away on very short words — `ja.lex.admin2.henkou` reached 4.8 s and
`ja.lex.health4.ukuu` 5.8 s against the `max(3.0, 1.2 + 0.35 × chars)` cap after
three resamples (`scripts/generate-pack-audio.py`); both produced clean clips on
the retry. Final parity: 4,894 manifest rows, 4,894 wav files, no row without
bytes and no bytes without a row. Pack gates after baking: `validate`, `lint`,
`test`, `audit-curriculum`, `audit-lexicon`, `audit-licenses` all **0 errors, 0
warnings**.

## Fields corrected during integration (engineering pass, not review)

The batch was checked against its own declared kana, and 23 fields disagreed
with it. Fixed in place; nothing here is a review verdict and every item stays
`status: draft`:

- **10 lexicon transliterations** did not transliterate the entry's own kana:
  `shopping2.kowaru` `kowaru`→`kowareru` (こわれる), `shopping2.tsuika`
  `tsuika`→`futsuka` (ふつか), `city2.hirosu` `hirosu`→`hirou` (ひろう),
  `admin2.nauhousho`→`nouhousho`, `admin2.kouhouryoukin`→`koukouryoukin`
  (こうこうりょうきん), `admin2.harai-komi` `harai-komi`→`furikomi` (ふりこみ),
  `admin2.juuminpyou`→`juuminhyou` (じゅうみんひょう), `admin2.kurejito`
  `kurejito`→`kurejitokado` (クレジットカード), `admin2.meinamba`→`mainambaa`
  (マイナンバー), `communication2.ryousai` `ryousai`→`ryoukai` (りょうかい).
  Romaji readings are learner-visible in romaji mode, so these were wrong on
  screen, not just in metadata.
- **8 sentence `romaji` fields** carried the same errors (including
  `ja.sent.a2.pharmacy.also-eyedrops`, whose kana reading is めぐすり while the
  romaji said `menyaku`).
- **5 sentence `reading` fields** contradicted their own `text`: だんぽボール →
  だんボール, けいさつ**に**とどけます → けいさつ**へ**とどけます (text says 警察へ),
  とどけ**で**したい → とどけ**を**したい, かぜぐすりを**さがさが**している →
  **さが**している, まいなんだー → マイナンバー. Audio follows: those five wavs
  were regenerated (`--force`) so the bytes match the corrected kana.
- **Id slugs keep the old wrong romaji** (`ja.lex.shopping2.tsuika`,
  `ja.lex.admin2.meinamba`, `ja.lex.health4.ukuu`, …). Ids are permanent keys
  referenced by units, sentence `content_refs` and media file names; the slug is
  not learner-visible. Renaming is a migration decision, listed here for the
  reviewer rather than taken silently.
- Reviewer still owes judgement on: the kanji spelling 十分钟 in
  `ja.sent.a2.move.takes-ten-minutes` (`text`), whether the pack teaches the
  short form くれじとカード (a sentence uses it) next to the lexicon form
  クレジットカード, and 警察へ vs に naturalness (flagged in that item's own notes).

## Machine-checkable defects outside this batch (measured, then repaired 2026-10-13)

Measured on the published pack, not on this batch:

- **510 of 4,057 ja-JP transliteration readings were malformed in shape** — the
  text contained a digit or kana, e.g. `ja.lex.abst2.ronso2` → `ronso2` for
  ろんそう, `ja.lex.abst2.kenso` → `kentoう`, `ja.lex.abst2.juri` → `jiゅnり`.
  All are pre-existing 0.3.0 content (mostly `lexicon/abst*`, `academic2`); 0 of
  them are in the seven pools added here.
- **67 `script`-labelled fields in 34 entries disagreed with their own text**
  (katakana words labelled `"script": "hiragana"` — マナー, ガイド, ランキング —
  plus one the other way). 16 are in the new pools, which followed the existing
  pack convention rather than inventing one.
- ru-RU declares 0 transliteration readings, so 0 rows are affected there.

The lint candidates landed as three `Severity::Error` codes —
`reading_transliteration_not_latin`, `romaji_contains_kana` and
`form_script_mismatch` — enforced by `audit-lexicon` **and** by `validate`, so
they block installation, not just CI. The rows were regenerated from each
entry's own kana by `scripts/fix-pack-transliterations.py`;
`audit-lexicon languages/ja-JP` now reports 0 errors and 0 warnings. Method,
before→after table and the rule derivation:
`state/evidence/PACK-LINT-2026-10-13/TRANSLITERATION-2026-10-13.md` and
`state/evidence/PACK-LINT-2026-10-13/transliteration-repair-2026-10-13.csv`.

### Next review task, highest value first

`state/evidence/PACK-LINT-2026-10-13/transliteration-review-2026-10-13.csv` lists the 98 entries whose
romaji passes the shape gate but no longer describes the entry: 60 carry the
English word where romaji belongs (びたみん → `vitamin`, マナー → `manner`,
ウェブサイト → `web-site`), and 38 have romaji and kana that are different words
(しびれ → `inmu`; `ja.lex.economy.rinkou` carries kana ちんぎん, romaji `rinkou`
and gloss "wages", so both fields are wrong). Each is a five-minute decision —
fix `romaji`, fix the kana reading, or reject the entry — backed by a
JMdict-Simplified check, which a machine may not self-certify. Changing a kana
reading also re-bakes that item's audio
(`scripts/generate-pack-audio.py --pack languages/ja-JP --only <id> --force`).

Separately, 621 well-formed rows follow the *other* romanisation convention —
collapsed long vowels and gemination (`koka`, `haken`, `shomei`) against the
newer `kouka`, `hakken`, `shomei`. Harmonising published rows is editorial
judgement, so it was not done mechanically; the regenerated values use the
spelled convention, and the `generated_conflict` column of the repair CSV names
the 84 rows where that now differs from another row's spelling of the same kana.

Agreement between a sentence's kanji `text` and its kana `reading` is only
partially machine-checkable: the five readings above were found because their
romaji disagreed with the kana, and a kana typo romanized consistently with
itself would not surface. Treat reading↔text agreement as a review task.
