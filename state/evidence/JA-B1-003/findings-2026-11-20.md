## Headline finding: the legacy B1 band is substantially corrupted, not merely shallow

The dominant defect class is **form↔gloss corruption**: the taught kana/kanji form and the
English gloss describe two different words (homophone/letter-swap collisions like
序列→"inferior", 報復→"response", 経理→"profit", 長身→"dieting"), or the form is a
nonexistent/garbled word outright (けたい, くろんじ, えねき, じょれつ→, イティネラリ).
This is the B1 scale of the same source-contamination class JA-A1-003 found at A1
(のぶん/"notebook"), and it is learner-visible: 193 P1 defects including 32 sentences that
teach the corrupted words in context (e.g. `じこ の しょうぎ を まじめ に かきました`
"the apology for the accident" — 将棋 is shogi).
