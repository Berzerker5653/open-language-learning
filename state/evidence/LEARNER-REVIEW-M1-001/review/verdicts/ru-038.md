# Verdict ru-038
Reviewer: fresh-context RU linguistic reviewer (Hermes subagent), 2026-11-27

Four stress-mark errors in vocabulary items: the locatives лесу́/саду́ carry end-stress, the partitive genitive of хлеб (substance) is хлеба́, and платить conjugates плачу́ — the marks shown change the intended word or violate the norm. Story sentences 893, 902-905, 907 are grammatical and idiomatic (905 is literary but correct). 909/910 are a stressed/unstressed duplicate pair, flagged once as known-limit; the same pattern continues in ru-039.

FLAG | item 895 | fix: "Прогу́лка в лесу́"
895: Locative of лес is stress-final: в лесу́; mark on the stem ("ле́су") is a definite stress error.
FLAG | item 897 | fix: "Ско́лько стои́т батон хлеба́?"
897: Substance genitive of хлеб is хлеба́; "хле́ба" is the plural (лоaves) — wrong form in "батон хлеба".
FLAG | item 906 | fix: "Ша́пка в саду́"
906: Locative of сад is stress-final: в саду́; mark on the stem ("са́ду") is a definite stress error.
FLAG | item 908 | fix: "Я плачу́ за проду́кты ка́рточкой."
908: платить → плачу́ (pay); "пла́чу" is the cry-sense of плакать — the stress mark changes the verb entirely.
FLAG | item 910 | known-limit
910: Duplicate of 909 (авто́бус/автобус, marked+unmarked); also 911/912 — likely intentional pair, verify dedupe.
