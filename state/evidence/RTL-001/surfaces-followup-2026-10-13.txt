RTL-001 follow-up — three shipped surfaces were missed by the first pass (2026-10-13)

Measured first (the gap):
  grep -n 'route === "' apps/desktop/src/App.tsx
    hub, pronunciation, dictation, shadowing, reading, words, placement, kanji,
    progress, writing, scenarios, settings
  grep -rn "PackText|usePackTextAttrs|LatinText" apps/desktop/src/App.tsx
    -> no matches: the kanji surfaces carried hand-rolled dir/lang attributes and
       the shadowing, reading and words routes carried none at all.
  So an ar-MA pack that installed and practised correctly still rendered its
  story titles, sentence text, looked-up words, shadowing targets and mined-word
  cards left-to-right. Graded reading also hardcoded nothing for romaji/gloss, so
  Latin scaffolding sat inside whatever direction its ancestor happened to have.

Why the missed routes were untestable where they were:
  apps/desktop/src/App.test.tsx header: "Mounting <App/> is not a seam this repo
  can drive: vitest here runs in node (no DOM dependency is pinned), and
  detectInvoke() returns null without window.__TAURI_INTERNALS__".
  The route JSX was inline inside App(), so no render test could reach it.

Change (matches the existing panel convention — practice.tsx, scenario.tsx,
dictation.tsx, pronunciation.tsx, hub.tsx are already separate panel modules):
  new apps/desktop/src/reading.tsx    (ReadingPanel + Story/StorySummary/StorySentence/StoryWord)
  new apps/desktop/src/shadowing.tsx  (ShadowingPanel + ShadowTarget)
  new apps/desktop/src/words.tsx      (MyWordsPanel + MineItem/MineDraft)
  App.tsx now renders the three panels and shares those types instead of
  repeating anonymous state types; the dead shadowCurrent derivation and the
  now-unused shadowTargetsState import were removed with the JSX.
  Direction applied: story title (inside the app's own h1), story button labels,
  sentence surface (Ruby keeps rendering bare text, the container carries dir),
  looked-up words, shadowing surface + pack-script reading, mined-word review
  prompt, mined-word list surfaces/readings, the surface and reading inputs.
  Latin scaffolding pinned dir="ltr": sentence romaji, sentence gloss, lookup
  romaji/gloss, a romanization-only shadowing scaffold. Learner-entered meaning
  and citation inputs deliberately carry neither dir nor lang.
  Same defect class, same pass: kanji prompt and kanji card moved from
  hand-rolled dir={activeRecord?.rtl…}/lang={activeRecord?.locale} to the shared
  PackText primitive, and phrase-slot text (task.slots) now carries direction.

Tests added (apps/desktop/src/rtl-direction.test.tsx, same ar-MA fixture as the
original pass, so the direction claims are all measured against one pack):
  - "flips story titles, sentences and looked-up words, but not the reading prose"
  - "names each story in the pack direction and leaves an LTR pack unflipped"
    (ja-JP control: <span lang="ja-JP"> and no dir="rtl" anywhere)
  - "flips the shadowing target and reading scaffold while romanization stays LTR"
  - "shows the romanization-only shadowing target left-to-right"
  - "carries pack direction through the review card and the add-word inputs"
