Residual false-positive classes (known, accepted): kakasi homograph kanji
readings (止める -> yameru vs expected とめる) and whisper mis-hearings of
very isolated short words. The bias is deliberate: churn (re-bake a good
clip) is preferred over a false PASS; every re-bake re-enters the same
gates.

## Defect classes observed (samples)

* TTS hallucinated vocalizations: `(笑)`, `*вздыхает*`, `Ха-ха-ха!`,
  `Уууу…` sustained vowels, `ふぅ…く…`.
* Mispronunciation: чек -> "Чик", пло́хо -> "ПЛАХА", цена́ -> "Сэна".
* Wrong/garbled content: ссылаться -> "Слаца!", достоверный -> "До свидания.",
  はんのう -> "I know", software-update sentences -> babble.
* Structure (segments): internal silence gaps > 0.8 s, pre-speech bursts.

then ja-JP, `VENV=.venv/bin/python` + whisper ggml-small. Synthesis is CPU

(`repair-r*.log`, `qa-r*.{jsonl,log}`, `qs-r*.jsonl`) and are summarized
here at campaign end, not committed.

## Gate v3 + judge decision (adviser-reviewed, 2026-10-22)

* ru gate is **phonemic**: both sides G2P'd with `espeak-ng -v ru --ipa`,
  stress marks stripped (whisper spelling is not a stress channel;
  prosody stays with the phoneme record layer + owner ears). rod/"Рот"
  now passes; ru v2->v3 840 -> 1,036 because real deviations previously
  hidden by orthographic matching are now visible.
* ja gate gains **JMDict reading rescue** (`ja_rescue`, single-word
  assets): kanji headword whose dictionary readings include the declared
  kana passes, recorded `rescued=jmdict`. Fixes kakasi homograph false
  positives (とめる/止める) without masking content errors (regression
  set: うみ/海(笑) and せん/せっ still fail).
* Final judge = **whisper ggml-large-v3-turbo** everywhere: ggml-small
  hallucinates on isolated short words (English bleed, kanji transcripts
  of kana speech), which dominated the ja failure list. Full-corpus
  turbo re-audits: `asr5-ru-RU.jsonl` (GamingPC) and
  `asr-turbo-ja-JP.jsonl` (Omarchy, then harvested here).

## Endgame: best-of-K, not more rounds

`scripts/bake-bestof.py`: per failing clip bake up to K takes (K=8 ru /
K=4-6 ja), grade EVERY take with the full gate, keep the first pass,
rewrite the manifest row with the attempt count ("best-of-K: take t of
up to K"). Clips that exhaust K get exactly K attempts on record and go
to the owner disposition list (RU-RUNDOWN section D: remove = remove the
content item; the media hole guard forbids silent deletion). Smoke
(K=3, stubborn core): rod pass@1, kod pass@3, med exhausted@3.
