# K-15 — Placement / diagnostic assessment

## What shipped

A low-stakes diagnosis pass that lets already-known material unlock
gated goals — the docs/03 §10 "can unlock known foundations" semantic,
built on the existing assessment schema (no new pack schema).

## Engine (`apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/lib.rs`)

- `placement_plan(profile_id)` → one item per pack assessment
  (`assessments/definitions.jsonl`): find the goal's unit
  (`curriculum/units/*.yaml`), take the first activity's
  `content_refs[0]` matching the assessment modality; map
  reading→recall-meaning, listening→comprehension-listening.
  Speaking/writing/interaction assessments are SKIPPED — no
  low-stakes auto-runner exists, and the diagnostic never fabricates
  evidence for unattempted constructs.
- `placement_submit` reuses the shared practice grading
  (`practice_submit_inner`) but tags the evidence
  `assessment_kind: Diagnostic` and attributes it to the assessment
  (`activity_id = assessment_id`). Normal practice attempts are now
  explicitly tagged `Formative`.
- Unlock is not a special case: diagnostic evidence flows through
  normal mastery derivation (policy fixture
  `known-diagnostic-reaches-independent`: two correct support-0
  diagnostic answers across prompts → `independent`), and the existing
  prerequisite gating (K-01) then admits the gated goal into the
  progression stream.

## UI (`apps/desktop/src/App.tsx`)

- "Placement" route + nav tab: two quick questions per assessment
  (the policy needs two correct answers across prompts), reusing
  `PracticeRunner` for the task UI; summary shows hits and states that
  known foundations unlock in Practice automatically.
