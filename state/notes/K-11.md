# K-11 — Shadowing (formative speak-along)

## What shipped

A shadowing loop: play model audio → speak along → record → play your own
recording back. Deliberately **formative only**: no analysis, no scoring,
no evidence rows, no scheduler state. With no calibrated pronunciation
scorer, any "feedback" would be fake; self-comparison is the honest
capability (docs/05 evidence rules).

## Engine

- `crates/activity/src/spec.rs`: `ActivityKind::Shadowing`; family names
  `["shadow", "shadowing", "echo"]`; `dimension_for(Shadowing, Speaking) =>
  ProductionSpeaking` exists **only** so a future calibrated path maps to
  the right dimension — the runtime never records through it (comment in
  code says so).
- `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/lib.rs`: `shadowing_items` /
  `shadowing_items_at` — targets are the active pack's lexicon entries
  (id/surface/reading/romaji), so any pack powers the activity. Registered
  in `invoke_handler`.
- Model audio reuses `speech_audio` (licensed `media/<content-id>.wav`
  first, TTS fallback, provenance-labeled).

## UI (`apps/desktop/src/App.tsx`)

- New `shadowing` route + nav tab.
- Section: surface + reading/romaji, "Play model audio", Record/Stop
  (reuses the in-memory WAV capture; nothing audio is persisted),
  "Play my recording" (blob URL), prev/next pager.
- `analyzeWav` returns early on the shadowing route — the recording is
  never sent to `pronunciation_check`.
- Banner states nothing is scored or saved.

## Decisions

- No scoring, no pitch display, no "accuracy" number: no calibrated
  scorer exists; fabricated feedback violates the evidence policy.
- Targets = whole pack lexicon rather than curriculum-filtered: simplest
  honest stream; scheduling shadowing would imply evidence semantics we
  refuse to grant.
